Introduction to the members

The Peruvian Network for Asia-Pacific Studies (REDAP) is made up of a diversity of public and private universities committed to the strategic study of the Asia-Pacific region. The institutions that form the network share a common vision: to strengthen academic cooperation, generate specialized knowledge and actively contribute to Peru’s international projection.

Each member contributes from its institutional strengths—research, training, regional outreach or innovation—to a collaborative platform that connects knowledge from different regions of the country. This plurality makes it possible to address the challenges of the Peru–Asia-Pacific relationship from an interdisciplinary, decentralized approach with real impact on public policy, talent development and academic diplomacy.

REDAP is more than an alliance: it is a living academic community, in constant dialogue with State actors, the private sector and international organizations, that works collectively to position Peru as a strategic interlocutor in the Asia-Pacific space.
