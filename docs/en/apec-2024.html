APEC Study Centers Conference 2024

APEC Peru 2024 · Lima

  • Overview
  • APEC 2008
  • APEC 2016
  • APEC 2024

The APEC Study Centers Consortium Conference 2024 (ASCCC 2024) was held in Lima, Peru, on August 22 and 23, 2024, as part of the Third Senior Officials’ Meeting (SOM3) of the APEC Forum. This academic event is part of the official activities of APEC 2024 and brings together, every year, researchers and scholars from the APEC Study Centers of the 21 member economies, with the aim of fostering academic debate and generating public policy inputs that contribute to regional economic integration.

Agenda and main topics

The academic agenda revolved around three strategic priorities set by the host country, Peru:

  • Trade and investment for inclusive and interconnected growth
  • Innovation and digitalization to promote the transition to the formal and global economy
  • Sustainable growth for resilient development

During the conference, thematic panels addressed these axes from different disciplines, aiming to enrich the dialogue between academia and policymakers.

International participation and representation

The event included representatives of APEC study centers from the 21 member economies, convened through REDAP, the local organizing entity. The call for papers received more than 30 academic proposals, selected by an evaluation committee according to their quality and relevance to the event’s priorities.

Delegates included scholars and researchers from the Americas, Asia and Oceania. Papers were presented by experts from Chile, Peru, Mexico, the United States, Canada, the Philippines, Korea, Russia and Papua New Guinea, among other countries.

Institutions represented included the University of Piura (academic chair of the consortium in 2024), the Center for China and Asia-Pacific Studies (CECHAP, University of the Pacific), the San Marcos Asian Studies Center (CEAS-UNMSM), Nankai University (China), Jeonbuk National University (Korea) and the Asia Pacific Foundation of Canada, among others. Altogether, more than 70 APEC-affiliated study centers make up the consortium, forming an active academic network across the region.

Nature of the participation

The conference was aimed mainly at researchers, scholars and specialists linked to the APEC Study Centers. It was not open to the general public, given its specialized nature. Participation required prior registration or a formal invitation from the organizing entities. Attendees included:

  • Speakers selected through the call for papers
  • Representatives of APEC centers
  • Forum officials
  • Institutional guests

The event thus became a high-level academic and institutional space, focused on knowledge exchange, comparative analysis and policy recommendations.

Previous editions and continuity of the consortium

  • The ASCCC is held every year in the country that holds the APEC Forum presidency. Each edition responds to the host’s priorities but keeps as its common thread the strengthening of regional academic cooperation.
  • In 2023, the conference was held at the University of California, Berkeley (United States), with topics such as international trade, economic inclusion and environmental sustainability.
  • In 2022, the venue was Phuket, Thailand, with an emphasis on post-COVID-19 recovery and regional economic integration.
