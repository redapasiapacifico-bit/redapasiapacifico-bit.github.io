REDAP in the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Forum (APEC)

REDAP is part of the APEC Study Centers Consortium and contributes to academic dialogue within the framework of the APEC Forum.

  • Overview
  • APEC 2008
  • APEC 2016
  • APEC 2024

Through its member universities and institutions, REDAP promotes independent research on trade, investment and economic cooperation, providing valuable inputs for public policy making and strengthening Peru’s academic presence in the Asia-Pacific region.

APEC Study Centers Consortium conferences in Peru

2008

Piura and Tumbes. ASCC 2008 conference, June 19 to 21.

2016

Arequipa. ASCC 2016 conference, between July and August.

2024

Lima. ASCCC 2024 conference, August 22 and 23.
