APEC Study Centers Conference 2008

APEC Peru 2008 · Piura and Tumbes

  • Overview
  • APEC 2008
  • APEC 2016
  • APEC 2024

As part of the official activities of APEC Peru 2008, the Peruvian Network for Asia-Pacific Studies (REDAP) organized the APEC Study Centers Consortium Conference (ASCC 2008) from June 19 to 21 in Piura and Tumbes. The event brought together distinguished scholars, officials and representatives of APEC centers from around the world, establishing Peru as a key player in generating knowledge on economic integration, trade and regional development in Asia-Pacific.

Agenda and main topics

ASCC 2008 addressed eight strategic topics for the region’s future. The sessions focused on:

  • Trade liberalization and facilitation in APEC
  • Trade costs and regional infrastructure
  • Structural reforms and SME development
  • Corporate social responsibility and sustainability
  • Regional institutional architecture
  • Higher education, innovation and student mobility
  • Human security, health, natural disasters and climate change
  • Asian regionalism and its trans-Pacific implications

These topics were discussed in joint sessions with LAEBA (IDB–ADBI) and PECC, connecting academic perspectives with regional public policy experience.

International participation and representation

The event brought together experts from APEC study centers, universities and multilateral organizations from Latin America and Asia-Pacific. Participants included representatives from New Zealand, Japan, China, Korea, Chile, Mexico, Australia, Colombia, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, India and the United States, as well as scholars from several Peruvian universities that are REDAP members.

Institutions such as the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB), the Asian Development Bank Institute (ADBI), the Pacific Economic Cooperation Council (PECC) and Peru’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs contributed key content to the program.

Nature of the participation

The conference was organized by REDAP with the support of Peruvian universities, ministries, the private sector and international partners. Participation was strictly academic and specialized, with selected papers and high-level thematic sessions. It included parallel activities such as institutional lunches, cultural dinners and a coordination meeting among the APEC Study Centers.

The event consolidated REDAP’s role as a bridge between Peruvian academia and APEC academic networks worldwide.

First edition

ASCC 2008 was the first time Peru hosted this important academic gathering during its APEC presidency. The experience set a precedent for future editions, strengthening the country’s academic presence in the Asia-Pacific region. Since this event, REDAP has expanded its active participation in the APEC Study Centers Consortium, contributing steadily to the consortium’s annual conferences and to regional academic dialogue.
